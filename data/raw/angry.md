Now the banners were already coming down. In the campus cafe, the barista asked if he wanted his usual, and Kwame said "No," louder than he meant to, and the room went quiet. He set a five-dollar bill on the counter, very precisely, and turned to leave. On the way out his foot caught a folding chair, which skidded across the tile with a shriek that made every head turn. He didn't apologize.
